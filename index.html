Selected work

Four engagements, from the most recent. Each opens to show the problem, what I did and what changed.

Data ingestion and quality remediation

Deloitte, 2023 to now

Context

A capital markets platform covering FX cash, forwards, futures, options, rates, swaps and bonds across APAC, EMEA and North America. Data quality issues meant teams were adjusting records by hand.

What I did

Owned functional delivery end to end. Wrote BRDs and FRDs in Confluence, used Oracle SQL to trace production issues and reconciliation breaks, and worked with risk teams to validate credit risk data. Ran requirement readiness for the Agile team.

Result

Average issue resolution time down 15 to 20%, less manual adjustment, steadier sprints with fewer spillover stories, and 10 to 15 releases a year supported.

Counterparty feed decommissioning

Ernst & Young, 2022 to 2023

Context

An upstream counterparty feed was being retired, and any downstream system that depended on it was at risk.

What I did

Led the impact analysis across downstream systems. Defined source-to-target mappings for regulatory datasets and acted as the link between business stakeholders and technology teams.

Result

Clearer traceability of capital markets data, and a safer path for the system enhancements that followed on trading and reporting platforms.

Analytics dashboards and process automation

Ernst & Young, 2022 to 2023

Context

Business users needed faster, more reliable insight into their data and operations.

What I did

Interviewed 20+ business users to gather requirements, then developed and maintained 10+ advanced analytics dashboards and automated key processes.

Result

Better data flow and decisions, with operational efficiency improved by 30%.

Sleeves accounting reconciliation

Broadridge, 2020 to 2022

Context

Portfolio accounting for investment sleeves needed securities, cash and transactions allocated correctly across each sleeve.

What I did

Analysed portfolio and sleeve accounting data, and used Python scripts on large financial datasets to find reconciliation breaks and resolve discrepancies.

Result

More accurate allocation and reporting, and a repeatable way to spot breaks in large datasets.
